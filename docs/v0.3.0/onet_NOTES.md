Job analysis, job descriptions, job specifications

CONTENT MODEL

- https://www.onetcenter.org/content.html


- Characteristics
   - Enduring traits that influence a person's perfomance on the job, abilities, interests, works styles and work values
- Requirements
   - Attributes acquired through experience and/or education, knowledge and skills
- Experience
   - Training and experience needed, job zone, education, training and experience
- Occupational
   - Actual work performed on the job, work activities
- Workforce
   - Labor market-related information, wages and employment
- Occupation
   - Detailed elements of specific jobs, tasks
