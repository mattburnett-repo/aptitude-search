# Script + storyboard (~45s)

**Tone:** clear, confident, no jargon wall.  
**CTA:** try the pipeline (paste a resume → get verified openings).

---

## Voiceover (full)

> Keyword job search finds titles. Aptitude Search finds fit.
>
> Paste your resume. We infer what you’re actually strong at—then map that to role families, not just the job titles on your CV.
>
> One pipeline runs end to end: profile, role plan, and a live search for open postings that match how you work.
>
> You get verified openings—real links, ranked by aptitude fit—not another keyword dump.
>
> Beyond the obvious job search. Try the pipeline.

**Word count:** ~95 · **pace:** ~125–130 wpm · **runtime:** ~45s
